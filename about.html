Welcome to Events by Delightful Decor, a luxury event planning company located in the heart of Boston. With over a decade of experience in the industry, our team is dedicated to creating unforgettable experiences for our clients. Our commitment to providing one‑on‑one consultation gives our clients dynamic visualizations of their ideas.

We offer full‑service event planning, meaning that we take care of everything from venue selection, catering, rentals, staffing, décor, floral design, and arrangements. We pride ourselves on being well‑versed in multiple cultures, with a deep understanding of the importance of cultural events. Every event is unique, and we work tirelessly to ensure that each client's vision is brought to life in a way that exceeds expectations.
