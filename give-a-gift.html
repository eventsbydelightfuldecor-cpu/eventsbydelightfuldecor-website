At Events by Delightful Decor, the team believes that everyone deserves to be celebrated and cherished. By offering our expertise and resources to charities, we are able to make a positive impact and bring joy to those who need it most.

The Give‑a‑Gift charity program is a reflection of Events by Delightful Decor's altruistic values and commitment to continuing this tradition of giving back. With the support of our clients and friends, we are able to create unforgettable events, provide gift baskets and treats that make a real difference in the lives of others.

If you would like to contribute to our Give‑a‑Gift program, please reach out and we will guide you through the process.
