Can you assist with event rentals?

Yes, we can assist with event rentals, including furniture, linens, lighting and décor. We have strong relationships with rental vendors and can negotiate the best prices and quality for our clients.

Can you work with a specific theme or concept?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating unique and personalized experiences that reflect our clients' visions and personalities. We can work with any theme or concept to create a one‑of‑a‑kind event.

Do you provide catering services?

We work with a network of reputable vendors to provide catering services that align with your event's theme and style. We can also assist with menu planning and offer recommendations for local chefs and restaurants.

How do you ensure that my vision comes to life?

Our team of skilled designers and craftsmen collaborate with you to understand your vision and translate it into a tangible reality that exceeds your expectations.

How far in advance should I book your services?

We recommend booking our services as early as possible to ensure availability. However, we understand that some events may be planned on short notice, and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.

How involved will I be in the planning process?

We believe in working closely with our clients throughout the planning process to ensure that every detail is executed to perfection. We will keep you involved every step of the way, from initial consultations to final walkthroughs.

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing structure varies depending on the complexity and size of the event. We offer a complimentary consultation to discuss your vision, goals and budget and provide a proposal with a detailed breakdown. We are committed to transparency and will work with you to stay within your budget while still creating a memorable and luxurious event.

What types of events does your company plan?

If you can think of it, we can create it!
